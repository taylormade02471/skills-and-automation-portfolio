## Proof 1: Nashville Live Audit Transit Planner

**Title**

Nashville Live Audit Transit Planner: Privacy-First Job Routing and Provider Sync

**Description**

I built and deployed a map-first field-work planner for Nashville job data. It separates open, accepted, needs-completion, and completed work; filters by provider; preserves job requirements; supports Android share intake; and lets the user select stops and build a route. Provider credentials remain outside the public web app, while connection and email surfaces use safe metadata and read-only permission boundaries.

Other AI / Automation, or Workflow Automation if that is the closest available category.

**Skills**

Workflow Automation, JavaScript, Node.js, Browser Automation, Data Pipelines & ETL, Git/GitHub, and Vercel deployment. Add AI Integration & APIs only when the proof form accepts connector-bound API boundary work.

**Outcome / ROI**

The planner turns scattered field-work records into a route-ready work queue with provider, pay, timing, address, status, and route decisions visible in one place. Do not enter a dollar or hours-saved claim until a before-and-after measurement is recorded.
